Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. What we collect

When you submit an inquiry form on this site, we collect your name, phone number, email address, preferred travel dates, number of travelers, and any message you provide. We do not collect payment details through this site.

2. How we use it

Your information is used solely to respond to your travel inquiry, follow up with relevant package information, and — if you opt in — send occasional updates about offers. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

3. Storage & security

Inquiry data is stored in our secured database and is only accessible to authorized JOY Makers staff via a password-protected admin panel. We use industry-standard measures including HTTPS encryption and rate-limited forms to protect submissions.

4. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing Joymakersholidays@gmail.com.

5. Cookies

This site may use minimal cookies for basic functionality. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Joymakersholidays@gmail.com.
